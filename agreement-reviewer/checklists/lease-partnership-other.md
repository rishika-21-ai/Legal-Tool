# Checklist: Lease, Partnership/JV, Sale & General

Apply **in addition to** `common-clauses.md`. Use the section that matches the
document; if it's a type not covered elsewhere, use the **General** section plus
common clauses.

Each entry: **Why it matters** · **Look for** · **Red flags** · **If missing**.

---

## Lease / rental (property or equipment)

### Rent, deposit & increases
- **Why it matters:** The core financial terms.
- **Look for:** Rent amount, due date, deposit amount and return conditions, and
  how/when rent can increase.
- **Red flags:** Uncapped or arbitrary rent increases; deposit with no return
  conditions.
- **If missing:** Add clear rent, deposit-return terms, and a cap/formula on
  increases.

### Term, renewal & early exit
- **Why it matters:** How long you're committed and how to leave.
- **Look for:** Term length, renewal option, break/early-termination rights, and
  holdover terms.
- **Red flags:** No break clause; punitive holdover rent; auto-renewal.
- **If missing:** Add renewal and early-exit terms.

### Maintenance & repairs
- **Why it matters:** Who fixes what.
- **Look for:** Split of responsibilities between landlord/lessor and tenant.
- **Red flags:** Tenant responsible for all structural/major repairs.
- **If missing:** Add a maintenance-responsibility split.

### Use, alterations & subletting
- **Why it matters:** What you may do with the property.
- **Look for:** Permitted use, rules on alterations, and sublet/assignment rights.
- **Red flags:** Overly narrow permitted use; no right to sublet even with consent.
- **If missing:** Add permitted-use and sublet/assignment-with-consent terms.

### Insurance & liability
- **Why it matters:** Who insures and who bears risk of damage/injury.
- **Look for:** Required insurance and a fair allocation of liability.
- **Red flags:** Tenant indemnifies landlord for the landlord's own negligence.
- **If missing:** Add balanced insurance and liability terms.

## Partnership / Joint Venture

### Capital contributions & ownership %
- **Why it matters:** Who puts in what and who owns what share.
- **Look for:** Each party's contribution and ownership/profit split.
- **Red flags:** Contributions or splits left "to be agreed."
- **If missing:** Define contributions and ownership percentages.

### Profit/loss sharing & distributions
- **Why it matters:** How money flows to the partners.
- **Look for:** Allocation of profits/losses and distribution timing.
- **Red flags:** Sharing unclear or not matching ownership.
- **If missing:** Add profit/loss allocation and distribution terms.

### Governance & decision-making
- **Why it matters:** How decisions get made and deadlocks resolved.
- **Look for:** Voting rights, reserved matters needing unanimity, and a deadlock
  mechanism.
- **Red flags:** One partner controls everything; no deadlock resolution.
- **If missing:** Add governance rules and a deadlock-breaking mechanism.

### Exit, buyout & dissolution
- **Why it matters:** How a partner leaves and how the venture ends.
- **Look for:** Buy-sell terms, valuation method, transfer restrictions, and
  wind-down process.
- **Red flags:** No exit route; no valuation method; a partner trapped in.
- **If missing:** Add buyout/valuation and dissolution terms.

### IP & non-compete between partners
- **Why it matters:** Who owns jointly created IP and whether partners can compete.
- **Look for:** Clear IP ownership and any agreed restrictions.
- **If missing:** Add IP ownership and any needed restrictions.

---

## Sale / Purchase of goods or assets

### Description, quantity & price
- **Look for:** Precise description, quantity, unit price, and total.
- **Red flags:** Vague description; price adjustable by seller alone.
- **If missing:** Add exact goods/asset description and pricing.

### Delivery, title & risk of loss
- **Look for:** Delivery terms/dates, when title passes, and when risk shifts.
- **Red flags:** Risk passes to buyer before delivery/inspection.
- **If missing:** Add delivery, title, and risk-transfer terms.

### Inspection, acceptance & returns
- **Look for:** A right to inspect and reject non-conforming goods.
- **Red flags:** Deemed acceptance immediately on delivery.
- **If missing:** Add an inspection/acceptance window.

### Warranties & remedies for defects
- **Look for:** Product warranty and remedies (repair/replace/refund).
- **Red flags:** "As is" with no remedy for defects.
- **If missing:** Add a warranty and defect remedies.

---

## General (any other agreement type)

Use `common-clauses.md` in full, then check:
- **Is the core commercial deal fully specified?** (what each side gives and gets,
  amounts, dates)
- **Is anything referenced but not attached?** (exhibits, schedules, SOWs)
- **Are obligations mutual where they should be?** (one-sided rights are the most
  common problem)
- **Is there a clear way out** (termination) **and a clear way to resolve
  disputes**?
- **Are the biggest risks capped** (liability) **and covered** (indemnity,
  insurance)?
