# Checklist: Service / Vendor / SaaS Agreement (incl. MSA)

Apply **in addition to** `common-clauses.md`. Clauses specific to service,
vendor, SaaS/subscription, and master service agreements.

Each entry: **Why it matters** · **Look for** · **Red flags** · **If missing**.

---

## Scope of services / description
- **Why it matters:** Defines exactly what is being delivered.
- **Look for:** A clear services description or an attached Statement of Work
  (SOW) / order form.
- **Red flags:** Vague scope ("services as agreed"); referenced SOW/exhibit not
  attached.
- **If missing:** Add or attach a specific description of services or deliverables.

## Fees, payment terms & taxes
- **Why it matters:** What you pay, when, and what happens if you don't.
- **Look for:** Amounts, invoicing schedule, payment window (e.g. net 30), late
  fees, and who bears taxes.
- **Red flags:** Vendor can change prices unilaterally; large late fees; payment
  due on receipt; unclear tax responsibility.
- **If missing:** Add clear fees, an invoicing/payment schedule, and a cap on
  any annual price increases.

## Service levels (SLA) / uptime
- **Why it matters:** Sets performance guarantees for SaaS/services.
- **Look for:** Uptime %, support response times, and **credits/remedies** if
  missed.
- **Red flags:** Uptime promised but no remedy if breached; credits so small they
  don't matter; SLA excluded entirely.
- **If missing:** Add an SLA with a measurable target and service credits as a
  remedy.

## Data ownership & return
- **Why it matters:** Confirms your data stays yours and you can get it back.
- **Look for:** Customer owns its data; export/return on termination in a usable
  format; deletion after a set period.
- **Red flags:** Vendor claims rights over customer data; no export on exit; data
  held hostage until final payment.
- **If missing:** Add "Customer owns all Customer Data; Vendor will make it
  available for export on termination and delete it thereafter."

## Data security
- **Why it matters:** How your data is protected.
- **Look for:** Security measures, breach notification, and relevant standards/
  certifications (e.g. SOC 2, ISO 27001).
- **Red flags:** No security commitments; no breach-notice obligation.
- **If missing:** Add security obligations and a prompt breach-notification duty.

## Subprocessors / subcontractors
- **Why it matters:** Third parties the vendor uses to handle your data or deliver
  services.
- **Look for:** Disclosure of subprocessors, flow-down of obligations, and notice
  of changes.
- **Red flags:** Unlimited right to subcontract with no accountability.
- **If missing:** Add a subprocessor clause requiring equivalent obligations and
  vendor responsibility for their acts.

## Support & maintenance
- **Why it matters:** How you get help and updates.
- **Look for:** Support channels, hours, and whether maintenance/updates are
  included.
- **Red flags:** Support only at extra cost with no detail; downtime for
  maintenance uncapped.
- **If missing:** Add support scope, hours, and maintenance-window limits.

## Term, renewal & auto-renewal
- **Why it matters:** Auto-renewal can lock you in and trigger surprise charges.
- **Look for:** Initial term, renewal length, and the **notice window** to cancel.
- **Red flags:** Auto-renews for a full year; long notice window (e.g. 90 days)
  to avoid renewal; price jumps on renewal.
- **If missing / risky:** Add a reasonable cancellation notice (e.g. 30 days) and
  cap renewal price increases.

## Warranties & acceptance
- **Why it matters:** Promise the service will perform as described, and a way to
  reject defective deliverables.
- **Look for:** A performance warranty and (for deliverables) an acceptance/
  rejection process.
- **Red flags:** Service provided "as is" with no warranty; deemed acceptance in
  an unrealistically short time.
- **If missing:** Add a performance warranty and a fair acceptance-testing period.

## Liability cap sizing (service-specific)
- **Why it matters:** Generic caps may be too low for the value at risk.
- **Look for:** A cap tied to fees (e.g. 12 months' fees) with carve-outs for
  confidentiality/IP/data breaches that sit above the cap.
- **Red flags:** Cap set at one month's fees; data-breach liability squeezed
  under a tiny cap.
- **If missing:** See `common-clauses.md` → Limitation of liability, and add
  super-cap carve-outs for data breach and confidentiality.

## Insurance
- **Why it matters:** Confirms the vendor can cover losses it causes.
- **Look for:** Required insurance types and minimum amounts.
- **Red flags:** No insurance requirement for a vendor handling sensitive data.
- **If missing:** Add reasonable insurance requirements (e.g. cyber, general
  liability).

## Exit / transition assistance
- **Why it matters:** Helps you move off the service without disruption.
- **Look for:** A transition/wind-down period and vendor cooperation on migration.
- **Red flags:** Service cuts off immediately on termination; no migration help.
- **If missing:** Add a transition-assistance clause with a defined wind-down
  period.
