# Sample Review (what the output looks like)

This is an example so you know what to expect. Here the user pasted a short,
one-way NDA and said they are the **receiving party** (the side getting the
confidential information). Your real reviews will follow this same 7-section
shape.

---

### 1. Agreement type & your position
This is a **one-way Non-Disclosure Agreement**. You are the **receiving party** —
you are getting the other side's confidential information and agreeing to protect
it.

### 2. Overall summary
The NDA is short and covers the basics, but it is **tilted toward the disclosing
party** and is missing several standard protections. The most important fix: it
has **no time limit and no standard exclusions**, so as written you'd have to
protect the information forever, even if it becomes public.

### 3. Missing clauses

| Clause | Why it matters | Suggested wording |
|---|---|---|
| Standard exclusions | Without them, you must protect info even after it becomes public or that you already knew. | "Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party, was already known to it, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party." |
| Term of confidentiality | There is no end date, so the duty is effectively perpetual. | "The confidentiality obligations continue for three (3) years from the date of disclosure." |
| Legally-required disclosure carve-out | You could be forced by law/subpoena to disclose and would breach the NDA. | "The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, provided it gives prompt notice where permitted." |
| Return or destruction | No clear duty for what happens to the info at the end. | "On written request, the Receiving Party will return or destroy all Confidential Information, except archival copies required by law." |

### 4. Present but risky / one-sided

| Clause | The issue | Severity | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-way obligation | Only you are bound. If you also share your own information, it's unprotected. | Medium | Make the NDA mutual if you will disclose anything of your own. |
| Definition of Confidential Information | Defined so broadly it covers "all information," including public data. | Medium | Limit it to non-public information disclosed for the stated purpose. |
| Injunctive relief | Only the disclosing party is granted the right to an injunction. | Low | Make the remedy mutual. |

### 5. Present & OK
- Parties are correctly named with a signature block.
- Purpose of disclosure is stated.
- Governing law is specified.

### 6. Questions to clarify
- Will you also be sharing your own confidential information? If yes, this should
  be a **mutual** NDA.
- Is there a specific transaction or relationship this supports, so the "purpose"
  can be tightened?

### 7. Reminder
This is a first-pass review to help you spot gaps — it is not legal advice. For
anything high-stakes, have a qualified lawyer confirm.
