Agreement Review Checklist

A free, self-service guide for reviewing agreements — no sign-in, no accounts, nothing to install.

⚠️ This checklist helps you spot gaps quickly. It is not legal advice. For anything high-stakes, have a qualified lawyer confirm.

Tip: what counts as “risky” or “one-sided” depends on your side — keep it in mind as you go. Read the agreement next to this list and mark each clause.
